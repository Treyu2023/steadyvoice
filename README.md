## Speak Well

Speak Well talks when she taps a button. No store, no account, nothing to pay.

## Put it on an Android phone

1. On her phone, open **Chrome** and go to this address:
   https://treyu2023.github.io/steadyvoice/
   She sees the Speak Well screen with big phrase buttons.
2. Tap **Install app** if Chrome shows it at the bottom of the screen.
   If not, tap the three dots **⋮** at the top right, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**), then **Install** or **Add**.
   She sees a small box that says Speak Well with its icon. After she taps Install, the box closes.
3. Go to the phone's home screen and tap the **Speak Well** icon.
   Speak Well opens full screen, without the Chrome address bar. Next time, tap that same icon.
   Do not look for it in the Play Store.
4. The first time, tap through **Show me how**. **Help** is on the home screen if she needs the steps again.

## What she can do

- Tap a phrase and the phone says the whole sentence.
- Type a sentence. Words she has said before are offered.
- Speak English out loud in Spanish, Italian, French, Chinese, or Japanese.
- Pick a voice, then set speed, pitch, and mood.
- Write a script in Notepad, pause it, and continue on the same line.
- Save address, license, car, hobbies, favorite color, people, and health in About me. Save a copy of my information makes a backup file; Put my information back restores it after a reinstall.
- Set a medicine time in About me, then Health. Tap Allow phone alerts and install the app. The phone can ring after Speak Well is closed. Tap the alert and it says the medicine twice.

## Phone calls

Tap Phone call at the top. On the phone's call screen, tap Speaker and turn the volume up. The other person hears her through the microphone.

## Voices

Free voices come from the phone. Android: Settings, search Text-to-speech, Google Text-to-speech, Install voice data.

## Medicine alarms: what to expect

The ring and vibration work on their own. Chrome on Android only lets a page speak after someone has tapped it, so when an alarm goes off before anyone has touched Speak Well, the spoken words can stay silent until she taps the screen or the alert. Tapping the alert opens Speak Well and says the medicine. If alerts do not appear while the app is closed, leave Speak Well open near medicine time; some phones block a closed app from ringing.

## On a Windows computer

On the GitHub page, click Code, then Download ZIP. Right-click the zip, click Extract All, then double-click Start-SpeakWell.bat in the new folder. A computer alarm rings only while that window is open.

Nothing she types is uploaded, except that if she picks another language, a sentence the app does not know may be sent to a translation service so it can be spoken (see privacy.html).
