## Speak Well

Send her this link. She opens it in Chrome:

https://raw.githack.com/Treyu2023/steadyvoice/main/index.html

Then she taps the three dots and **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**. Next time she taps the Speak Well icon. No store and no account.

## What she can do

- Tap a phrase and the phone says the whole sentence.
- Type a sentence. Words she has said before are offered.
- Speak English out loud in Spanish, Italian, French, Chinese, or Japanese.
- Pick a woman's or a man's voice, then set speed, pitch, and mood.
- Write a script in Notepad, pause it, and continue on the same line.
- Save address, license, car, hobbies, favorite color, people, and health in About me.
- Set a medicine time. Allow alerts and install the app. The phone can ring after Speak Well is closed. Tap the alert and it says the medicine twice.
- Help, on the home screen, has the setup steps. Show me how walks through them.

## If a link is hard

Send SpeakWell.html, or SpeakWell.zip for a Windows computer. HOW-TO-INSTALL.txt is the plain-language version.

Nothing she types is uploaded. A backup file puts it back after a reinstall.
