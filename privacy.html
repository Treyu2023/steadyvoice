Speak Well

Speak Well is a talking aid. She taps a button or types, and the phone speaks.

What stays on the phone

Phrases, notes, conversations, voice choices, and About me are saved only on that phone. About me can include a name, address, license number, people, health notes, and medicine times. Speak Well does not have an account and does not upload that information.

What leaves the phone

If she turns on another language, an unknown sentence may be sent to a translation service so it can be spoken. Phrases the app already knows are translated on the phone and are not sent. Medicine alarms, speech, and the phrase buttons do not need an account.

Who it is for

Speak Well is a communication aid. It is not a doctor and it does not decide a dose. The medicine alarm only reminds her of a time she entered.
