HOW TO SET UP STEADYVOICE

SteadyVoice speaks when she taps a word.
You do not need a store.
You do not need an account.
You do not pay.

The phone is the main way.
A Windows computer is the other way.

================================
WHAT IT DOES
================================

Home is a short list. Tap one thing.

Say a phrase
  Big buttons. The phone says the whole sentence.

Type a sentence
  For words that are not on a button.
  After she uses the app, it offers words she has already said.
  It does not guess from a big dictionary.

Phone call
  Tap Phone call at the top.
  On the phone's call screen, tap Speaker.
  Turn the volume up.
  Hold the phone a little away.
  The other person hears her through the microphone.

Conversations
  What she says is kept on the phone, in order.
  She can open an old talk and say it again.
  Save and start a new talk when the subject changes.

Voices and look
  The screen starts dark.
  Speed and pitch are on Type a sentence. Free high-quality voices come from the phone:
  Android Settings, search Text-to-speech, Google Text-to-speech, Install voice data.
  iPhone Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices.
  Mood goes from soft to harsh.
  Sweet, Giggle, Drama, and Grumpy are for play.
  Notepad
  Three notes stay on the phone. She can write a conversation ahead of time,
  speak it, pause on a line, use another button, then continue where she stopped.

About me
  Address, license, plate, people, health, and short reminders.
  Typing "my address" or a person's name offers the real words.
  Save a copy of my information from that screen.
  After a reinstall, choose Put my information back and pick that same file.

Arrange is locked until someone opens it, so buttons are not moved by accident.

Emergency
  Call 911 and Emergency are not on the front.
  Open Emergency only when she means it.

Show me how
  Four short steps. She can open them again from Home.

Nothing she says is uploaded.
A backup is in Voices and look, then Install and backup.

================================
ON HER ANDROID PHONE
================================

You only need one file: SteadyVoice.html.
The other files are for a Windows computer.
Do not tap those on the phone.

1. Send SteadyVoice.html to the phone.
   A text or an email is fine.

2. On the phone, tap the file.
   If the phone asks how to open it, tap Chrome.
   Do not use a different browser.
   If Chrome is not there, install Google Chrome
   from the Play Store. Then try again.

3. If the file only downloads, open Chrome.
   Tap the three dots at the top.
   Tap Downloads.
   Tap SteadyVoice.html.

4. Put the icon on the home screen.
   With the page open, tap the three dots.
   Tap Add to Home screen.
   If you see Install app, tap that instead.
   Tap Add.
   The name should say SteadyVoice.

5. Leave the file in Downloads.
   Do not move it.
   Do not delete it.
   The home screen icon needs that file.

6. It works with no internet.
   The app is the file on the phone.
   After it opens once, Wi-Fi can be off.
   If it will not speak, turn Wi-Fi on and try once.
   Then Wi-Fi can be off again.

7. Next time, tap the SteadyVoice icon.

If you do not see Add to Home screen:
Open Chrome.
Tap the three dots.
Tap Downloads.
Tap SteadyVoice.html.
It still works.

Do not open the file from inside the zip.
Take SteadyVoice.html out first.

================================
ON A WINDOWS COMPUTER
================================

1. Find SteadyVoice.zip.

2. Right-click the zip.
   Click Extract All.
   Click Extract.
   A folder named SteadyVoice opens.

3. Use that new folder.
   Do not start it from inside the zip window.

4. Double-click Start-SteadyVoice.bat.
   Do not double-click SteadyVoice.html.
   Do not double-click the other Start-SteadyVoice file.

5. If Windows asks "Do you want to open this file?",
   click Open.

6. A black box may flash. That is okay.
   SteadyVoice then opens in its own window.
   There is no address bar. That is right.

7. If a box says Chrome or Edge was not found:
   Ask someone to install Google Chrome.
   Then double-click Start-SteadyVoice.bat again.

Next time:
Open the SteadyVoice folder.
Double-click Start-SteadyVoice.bat again.

Keep all the files in that same folder.
If you move only one file, it will not open.
