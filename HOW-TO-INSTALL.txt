HOW TO SET UP SPEAK WELL

Speak Well talks when she taps a button.
You do not need a store.
You do not need an account.
You do not pay.

================================
THE EASY WAY, ON HER PHONE
================================

1. Open this link in Chrome.
   https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/Treyu2023/steadyvoice@main/index.html

2. Tap the three dots at the top.

3. Tap Install app.
   If you do not see Install app, tap Add to Home screen.
   Tap Add.

4. The icon says Speak Well.
   Next time, tap that icon.
   Do not look for it in the Play Store.

5. The first time, tap through Show me how.
   Help is on the home screen if you need the steps again.

================================
WHAT SHE CAN DO
================================

Say a phrase
  Big buttons. The phone says the whole sentence.

Type a sentence
  For words that are not on a button.
  Words she has already said are offered.

Notepad
  Three notes stay on the phone.
  She can write a conversation ahead of time, pause, then continue on the same line.

About me
  Address, license, plate, car, hobbies, favorite color, people, and health.
  Diabetic is Yes, No, or Not sure.
  Typing "my address" or a person's name offers the real words.
  Save a copy of my information.
  After a reinstall, choose Put my information back and pick that same file.

Medicine alarm
  About me, then Health.
  Add the medicine name, the amount, and the time.
  Tap Allow phone alerts. Tap Allow.
  Install Speak Well, using the steps above.
  At that time the phone can ring and vibrate even if Speak Well is closed.
  Tap the alert. Speak Well opens and says the medicine out loud, twice.
  Tap I took it when she has taken it.
  Test alarm plays it once, so you can hear it before the real time.
  If the alert does not appear while the app is closed, leave Speak Well open near medicine time. Some phones block a closed app from ringing.

Phone call
  Tap Phone call at the top.
  On the phone's call screen, tap Speaker.
  Turn the volume up.
  Hold the phone a little away.
  The other person hears her through the microphone.

Voices
  The screen starts dark.
  Speed and pitch are on Type a sentence.
  Free voices come from the phone.
  Android: Settings, search Text-to-speech, Google Text-to-speech, Install voice data.
  iPhone: Settings, Accessibility, Spoken Content, Voices.

Emergency
  Call 911 and Emergency are not on the front.
  Open Emergency only when she means it.

Nothing she says is uploaded.

================================
IF A LINK IS HARD
================================

Send SpeakWell.html only.
A text or an email is fine.

1. On the phone, tap the file.
   If the phone asks how to open it, tap Chrome.

2. If the file only downloads, open Chrome.
   Tap the three dots.
   Tap Downloads.
   Tap SpeakWell.html.

3. Add it to the home screen the same way as above.

Leave the file in Downloads.
A file opened this way can speak and ring while Speak Well is open.
The alarm after the app is closed needs the link, installed, with alerts allowed.

================================
ON A WINDOWS COMPUTER
================================

1. Find SpeakWell.zip.

2. Right-click the zip.
   Click Extract All.
   Click Extract.
   A folder named SpeakWell opens.

3. Use that new folder.
   Do not start it from inside the zip window.

4. Double-click Start-SpeakWell.bat.
   Do not double-click the html file.

5. If Windows asks "Do you want to open this file?", click Open.

6. Speak Well opens in its own window.

Next time, open the SpeakWell folder and double-click Start-SpeakWell.bat again.
Keep all the files in that same folder.
A computer alarm rings while the window is open. It cannot ring after the window is closed.
